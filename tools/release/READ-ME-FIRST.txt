Wonder Boy in Monster World: native PC port (Windows, 64-bit)

This download has no game data. You need your own dump of the cartridge.

1. Make a folder called rom next to wbmw.exe.
2. Put your dump in it, named exactly:

       Wonder Boy in Monster World (USA, Europe).md

   It must be the USA/Europe version: 786,432 bytes, with SHA-1
   87a968f773c7e807e647c0737132457b06b78276. Another version will not work right.

3. Run wbmw.exe.

It starts full screen. Esc opens the menu, with the settings and the controls. Settings are kept in
wbmw.ini, and saves next to the game.

Source, documentation and how to build it:
https://github.com/matthewkris/wbmw-decomp

The licences are in the licences folder. Nothing here grants any rights in the original game.
